3 Where in the home is this?

Location matters — kitchen and bath work needs GFCI protection, garage work is often EV or generator work.

4 Your panel & service — best you know

The panel is the grey box usually in the garage, laundry or outside, with rows of breakers inside. “Not sure” is a perfectly good answer — Sharma checks it in person.

And how does power reach your home?

Look outside: do wires run from a street pole to a pipe (a mast) on your roof or wall — or does everything come in underground?

5 A little about your home

Older homes were built for far fewer appliances — age and new big loads help Sharma judge panel size before the visit.

Added anything big lately — or planning to?

New big loads are the #1 reason panels run out of room.
